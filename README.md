# Union-Castle Round Africa — Ocean Liner Journey Map

An interactive map of the Union-Castle Line's "Round Africa" mail and
intermediate services, reconstructed from the company's **June 1955 – May 1956**
timetable and route map.

Click any port to see sailing times to its neighbours, or switch to
**Plan a journey** mode to pick an origin and destination and have the map
trace the route and total the days at sea (with a toggle for the longer way
round the loop).

## Viewing

- **[`index.html`](index.html)** — vintage nautical-chart styling (default)

This is a self-contained single HTML page. Open in a browser, or view
the hosted version via GitHub Pages.

## About the data

The figures are **sailing time between ports** — the gap between a vessel's
departure from one port and its arrival at the next — and **exclude the days a
ship spent in port**. They are derived from the dates printed in the 1955–56
timetable, which itself notes that "dates and ports of call are approximate".
Each service's timings are taken from one representative voyage:

- **Suez / East Coast service** — *Dunnottar Castle* (outbound, June 1955)
- **West Coast mail service** — *Durban Castle* (homeward, July 1955)
- **Intermediate West Coast service** — *Bloemfontein Castle* (May 1955)

The route lines are **approximate sea tracks** drawn to follow coastlines and
shipping lanes; the source does not record actual courses.

## Source

Union-Castle Line, *Round Africa Services* timetable, June 1955 – May 1956.
